Privacy Policy

Your data is your data.

1. Information Collection

AutoFlow is built on the philosophy of a local-first deployment orchestrator. We do not collect, transmit, or store your SSH keys, deployment credentials, source code, or server IP addresses. All operations happen directly between your local machine and your servers.

2. Telemetry

We collect anonymized, opt-in telemetry to improve the stability of the desktop application. This includes generic crash reports and basic feature usage statistics. You can disable this at any time in the settings menu. We never collect personally identifiable information.

3. Third-Party Services

We do not share any data with third parties. We don't even have your data to share in the first place.

4. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, our data practices, or need support with the AutoFlow desktop client, please contact us at info@primetech.live or visit our Support Page.
